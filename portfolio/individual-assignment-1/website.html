Little Luxuries

Giving my favorite pieces a new home while putting the proceeds toward my college tuition.

 

Click here to see jewelry types

 

My jewelry collection is filled with pieces I've collected over the years, including rings, pendants, bracelets, and colorful gemstones. What I love most about the collection is the variety of styles, colors, and materials. Some pieces are simple and dainty, while others are much more unique and eye-catching. Together, they create a collection that feels personal and one of a kind.

Although it's difficult to part ways with pieces that I've collected and enjoyed, I have decided to give them a new purpose. I am selling my collection to help put money toward my college tuition and invest in my education. Each piece can find a new home while helping me work toward my goals that's important to my future.

There's a little bit of everything in this collection, which is what makes it feel so special to me. I hope these pieces can find new homes with people who will enjoy wearing them as much as I have. And knowing the money is going toward school makes letting go of some of my favorites a little easier!

Reasons why my collection is special

  • These pieces have been collected from all over the world
  • It has a lot of variety
  • This collection reflects my personal style over time
