The Buzz Behind the Bean

 

Caffeinated coffee beans naturally contain caffeine, which is one of the main reasons people drink coffee for an extra boost of energy. The amount of caffeine can vary depending on the type of coffee bean, how it's roasted, and how the coffee is brewed. Caffeinated coffee also keeps the full flavor and natural notes of the bean because it doesn't go through the extra process used to remove caffeine.

Did You Know?

  • Caffeine occurs naturally in coffee beans before they're roasted.
  • Robusta beans usually cointain more caffeine than Arabica beans.
  • Brewing method matters because different methods can change how much caffeine ends up in your cup.
  • Caffeine can help increase alterness and make you feel more awake.
  • Roast level doesn't automatically determine caffeine strength. A darker flavor doesn't always mean more caffeine.
