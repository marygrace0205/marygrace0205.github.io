All About Decaf

 

Decaf coffee starts with regular coffee beans, but most of the caffeine is removed before the beans are roasted. Even though it contains much less caffeine, decaf coffee can still have a rich flavor and many of the same characteristics as regular coffee. Different methods can be used to remove the caffeine, and a small amount may still remain in the finished cup.

Did You Know?

  • Decaf coffee isn't completely caffeine-free. An 8-ounce cup typically contains about 2-15mg. of caffeine, compared with roughly 80-100mg. in a regular cup of coffee.
  • Most of the caffeine is removed before the beans are roasted.
  • Water, carbon dioxide, or other methods can be used during the decaffeination process.
  • Decaf coffee still contains many of the same natural flavors as regular coffee.
  • It can be a good option for people who enjoy coffee, but want less caffeine.
